# Aqeedah Reference — Manhaj Salaf

Core creed (aqeedah) topics within the Salafi methodology.

> **Important distinction:** The Shafi'i preference in this skill applies to **fiqh (jurisprudence)** only. In **aqeedah (creed)**, the Salafi methodology is followed exclusively — based on Al-Quran, As-Sunnah, and the understanding of the Salafush Shalih. Imam Shafi'i himself (rahimahullah) was firmly upon the aqeedah of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah.

---

## TAWHID (Monotheism) — The Foundation of Islam

### Three Categories of Tawhid

1. **Tawhid Ar-Rububiyyah** — Oneness of Allah's Lordship: affirming that Allah alone is the Creator, Sustainer, and Controller of all affairs
2. **Tawhid Al-Uluhiyyah** — Oneness of Worship: all acts of worship must be directed to Allah alone
3. **Tawhid Al-Asma' was Sifat** — Oneness of Names and Attributes: affirming Allah's names and attributes as they appear in the texts, without distortion (tahrif), negation (ta'til), resemblance to creation (tamthil), or asking "how" (takyif)

**Key source:** Kitab At-Tawhid by Shaykh Muhammad bin Abdul Wahhab (rahimahullah)

---

## SHIRK (Associating Partners with Allah)

### Categories

- **Shirk Akbar (Major Shirk)** — Exits the person from Islam. Examples: supplicating to other than Allah, making vows to graves, seeking intercession through the deceased
- **Shirk Asghar (Minor Shirk)** — Does not exit from Islam but carries severe warning. Examples: riya' (showing off in worship), swearing by other than Allah

### Common Modern Forms

- Visiting graves and making du'a to the deceased (tawassul through the dead)
- Wearing amulets (ta'wiz/jimat) with the belief they have inherent power
- Seeking ruqyah from practitioners using non-Quranic or impermissible methods

**Key fatwa:** Lajnah Da'imah (alifta.net); Shaykh Ibn Baz (binbaz.org.sa)

---

## BID'AH (Religious Innovation)

### Definition

Introducing into the religion anything that Allah and His Messenger did not legislate, whether in belief or practice.

**Daleel:** The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Every innovation is misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire."* (HR. An-Nasa'i — graded sahih by Al-Albani)

### Common Bid'ah in Indonesia

- **Tahlilan** — Commemoration gatherings held on the 3rd, 7th, 40th, or 100th day after a death
- **Maulid celebrations** with specific rituals that have no authenticated basis in the Sunnah
- **Qunut Nazilah** recited in all five daily prayers without a specific calamity
- **Congregational dhikr** after obligatory prayers with a designated leader and fixed count

**Reference:** Almanhaj.or.id; Muslim.or.id; Ustaz Yazid bin Abdul Qadir Jawas (rahimahullah)

---

## AHLUS SUNNAH WAL JAMA'AH — Correct Understanding

The "saved group" (firqah najiyah) refers not to any specific organization, but to those who follow:

1. The Quran
2. The authentic Sunnah
3. Upon the understanding of the Sahabah (Companions of the Prophet ﷺ)

This is a methodology, not a party or movement.

---

## IMAN (Faith) — Its Pillars and Nature

### The Six Pillars of Iman

1. Belief in Allah
2. Belief in His Angels
3. Belief in His Books
4. Belief in His Messengers
5. Belief in the Last Day
6. Belief in Qadar (Divine Decree) — both its good and its evil

### Iman Increases and Decreases

The Salafi position: Iman increases through acts of obedience and decreases through sins. This is the agreed-upon position of the Salaf, in opposition to the Murji'ah who claim iman does not fluctuate.

---

## AL-WALA' WAL BARA' (Loyalty and Disavowal)

- **Al-Wala'** — Love for Allah, His Messenger ﷺ, and the believers; supporting them
- **Al-Bara'** — Disavowal of shirk, kufr, and bid'ah — directed at the falsehood itself, not necessarily at individuals as people

**Important distinction:** Disavowing kufr and innovation does not mean being unjust to non-Muslims or preventing da'wah being given to them. Muslims must still deal with others fairly and with good character.

<example>
English. The Shafi'i position aligns with strong daleel.

**Q: Is celebrating birthdays allowed in Islam?**

*Bismillah.*

**Daleel (Evidence):**
The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Whoever introduces into this affair of ours something that does not belong to it, it will be rejected."* (HR. Bukhari no. 2697, Muslim no. 1718 — muttafaq 'alayh (agreed upon by Bukhari and Muslim), sahih)

**Positions:**
Neither the Shafi'i madhab nor the broader Salafi scholarship has any basis for birthday celebrations. Shaykh Ibn Baz (rahimahullah) and Shaykh Al-Fawzan have stated it is a bid'ah with no precedent from the Prophet ﷺ or his Companions. (islamqa.info fatwa no. 1027)

**Practical guidance:**
A Muslim should leave birthday celebrations and replace them with righteous deeds — du'a, charity, and gratitude to Allah.

*Wallahu a'lam.*
</example>

<example>
English. The Shafi'i position is noted alongside stronger daleel.

**Q: Does touching a woman (non-mahram) nullify wudhu?**

*Bismillah.*

**Daleel (Evidence):**

- Quran 4:43 (*"aw laamastum al-nisaa'"*), as interpreted by Imam Shafi'i.
- The Prophet ﷺ kissed some of his wives and then prayed without renewing wudhu. (HR. Abu Dawud no. 179 — graded sahih by Al-Albani)

**Positions:**

- *Shafi'i (mu'tamad):* In the relied-upon (mu'tamad) position of the Shafi'i madhab, touching the skin of a non-mahram woman — even without desire — nullifies wudhu. This is based on the apparent meaning of Quran 4:43 (*"aw laamastum al-nisaa'"*) as interpreted by Imam Shafi'i. (Reference: *Minhaj Al-Talibin*, Imam Nawawi)
- *Stronger daleel (alternative position):* However, many scholars, including Shaykh Al-Albani (rahimahullah) and Ibn Hazm, held that skin contact alone does not nullify wudhu unless accompanied by desire or actual sexual discharge. Their evidence: the Prophet ﷺ kissed some of his wives and then prayed without renewing wudhu. (HR. Abu Dawud no. 179 — graded sahih by Al-Albani; see also islamqa.info fatwa no. 2178, citing Fataawa al-Lajnah al-Daa'imah 5/266, which says touching one's wife does not nullify wudhu with or without desire)

**Practical guidance:**
If you follow the Shafi'i madhab, renew your wudhu after such contact. If you adopt the daleel-based opinion of Al-Albani and others, skin contact without desire does not require renewal. Both positions are held by recognized scholars. The more cautious (ihtiyat) approach is to follow the Shafi'i position.

*Wallahu a'lam.*
</example>

<example>
Indonesian. Same shape, headings in Indonesian.

**Q: Apakah memegang wanita bukan mahram membatalkan wudhu?**

*Bismillah.*

**Dalil:**

- QS. An-Nisa: 43 (*"aw laamastum al-nisaa'"*), sebagaimana ditafsirkan Imam Syafi'i.
- Nabi ﷺ pernah mencium sebagian istrinya lalu shalat tanpa memperbarui wudhu. (HR. Abu Dawud no. 179 — disahihkan Al-Albani)

**Pendapat ulama:**

- *Madhab Syafi'i (mu'tamad):* Dalam pendapat yang dipegang (mu'tamad) dalam Madhab Syafi'i, menyentuh kulit wanita bukan mahram — meski tanpa syahwat — membatalkan wudhu. Dalilnya adalah zahir firman Allah dalam QS. An-Nisa: 43 (*"aw laamastum al-nisaa'"*) sebagaimana ditafsirkan Imam Syafi'i. (Rujukan: *Minhaj Al-Talibin*, Imam Nawawi)
- *Pendapat yang lebih kuat berdasarkan dalil:* Namun, sejumlah ulama — di antaranya Syaikh Al-Albani (rahimahullah) dan Ibnu Hazm — berpendapat bahwa sentuhan kulit semata tidak membatalkan wudhu kecuali disertai syahwat atau keluarnya sesuatu. Dalilnya: Nabi ﷺ pernah mencium sebagian istrinya lalu shalat tanpa memperbarui wudhu. (HR. Abu Dawud no. 179 — disahihkan Al-Albani; lihat juga islamqa.info fatwa no. 2178, yang mengutip Fataawa al-Lajnah al-Daa'imah 5/266, bahwa menyentuh istri tidak membatalkan wudhu baik dengan syahwat maupun tanpa syahwat)

**Panduan praktis:**
Jika Anda mengikuti Madhab Syafi'i, perbaruilah wudhu setelah menyentuh wanita bukan mahram. Jika mengambil pendapat Al-Albani dan ulama lain yang serupa, sentuhan tanpa syahwat tidak membatalkan wudhu. Kedua pendapat ini dipegang oleh ulama yang diakui. Sikap yang lebih berhati-hati (ihtiyat) adalah mengikuti posisi Syafi'i.

*Wallahu a'lam.*
</example>

# references/fiqh-ibadah.md

# Fiqh Al-Ibadah Reference — Acts of Worship (Manhaj Salaf + Shafi'i)

Core worship rulings. The **Shafi'i position is presented first** as the default. Where the Shafi'i position is weak or superseded by stronger daleel, the stronger opinion is clearly noted.

---

## SALAH (Prayer)

### Conditions for a Valid Prayer

1. Islam
2. Sanity (aql)
3. Reaching puberty (baligh) — children should be taught from age 7 and instructed firmly by age 10
4. Entering the prayer time
5. Ritual purity (taharah)
6. Facing the Qiblah
7. Covering the awrah (private parts)

### Key Positions on Prayer — Shafi'i First

**Basmalah (Bismillah) recitation:**

- **Shafi'i (mu'tamad):** Recite Bismillah aloud in jahriyyah prayers (Fajr, Maghrib, Isha) — it is a verse of Al-Fatihah and must be recited. (Imam Nawawi, *Al-Majmu'*)
- **Salafi/Hanbali:** Basmalah is recited quietly — the stronger hadith evidence supports silent recitation. (Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen; HR. Muslim no. 399 supports quiet recitation)
- *Note: Both are valid scholarly positions; the Shafi'i position is the mu'tamad for those following this madhab.*

**Qunut in Fajr:**

- **Shafi'i (mu'tamad):** Qunut Al-Fajr after ruku' in the second rak'ah is sunnah, consistently practiced. (*Minhaj Al-Talibin*; *Fath Al-Mu'in*)
- **Salafi/majority:** No consistent qunut in Fajr — the hadith evidence for it is weak; Qunut Nazilah only during specific calamities. (Shaykh Al-Albani; Shaykh Ibn Baz)
- *Note: This is a well-known ikhtilaf. The Shafi'i position is well-supported within the madhab. Salafi scholars prefer abandoning it based on hadith grading.*

**Raising the hands (Raf' al-Yadayn):**

- **Shafi'i and Salafi (agreed):** Sunnah at the opening takbir, before and after ruku' — authenticated in Bukhari and Muslim. Both agree on this.

**Placing hands on chest during standing (qiyam):**

- **Shafi'i:** Hands placed on chest/upper abdomen area. (*Al-Umm*)
- **Salafi:** Same position — based on hadith of Sahl ibn Sa'd (HR. Bukhari). Agreement on this.

**Touching non-mahram skin and wudhu:**

- **Shafi'i (mu'tamad):** Skin contact with non-mahram woman nullifies wudhu. (*Minhaj Al-Talibin*)
- **Stronger daleel position:** Contact without desire does not nullify wudhu — supported by practice of the Prophet ﷺ. (Al-Albani: HR. Abu Dawud no. 179 — graded sahih by Al-Albani; see also islamqa.info fatwa no. 2178, citing Fataawa al-Lajnah al-Daa'imah 5/266, which says touching one's wife does not nullify wudhu with or without desire)

**Praying behind a sinner or innovator:** Permissible with conditions; avoid where it normalizes bid'ah.

**Reference:** *Minhaj Al-Talibin* (Imam Nawawi); *Fath Al-Mu'in*; Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen, *Ash-Sharh Al-Mumti'* (ibnothaimeen.com)

---

## ZAKAT (Obligatory Almsgiving)

### Nisab Thresholds

- **Gold:** 85 grams
- **Silver:** 595 grams
- For cash and trade assets, use the silver nisab — it is the more conservative and protective standard for zakat recipients
- **Rate:** 2.5% annually on zakatable wealth that has been held for a full lunar year (hawl)

### Zakat Al-Fitr

- **Amount:** 1 sha' (~2.5 kg) of the local staple food (rice in Indonesia)
- **Deadline:** Must be paid before the Eid Al-Fitr prayer
- **Paying in cash:** Scholarly ikhtilaf exists — Shaykh Ibn Baz and the majority prefer food; some Indonesian scholars permit cash payment for practical reasons

---

## SAWM (Fasting — Ramadan)

### Actions That Invalidate the Fast

1. Eating or drinking intentionally
2. Sexual intercourse
3. Intentional vomiting
4. Menstruation (haidh) or post-natal bleeding (nifas)
5. Apostasy (riddah)

### Common Fasting Questions

- **Injections / IV drips:** Nutritive IV breaks the fast; purely medicinal (non-nutritive) injections — scholarly ikhtilaf; majority say they do not break the fast
- **Using a toothbrush or miswak:** Permissible; avoid swallowing toothpaste
- **Watching prohibited content:** Does not break the fast physically but is sinful and negates the spiritual reward of fasting

**Reference:** islamqa.info; Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen's fatawa on fasting

---

## HAJJ & UMRAH

- Hajj is obligatory once in a lifetime for every Muslim with the physical and financial ability (istita'ah)
- Umrah is a highly recommended Sunnah
- **Visiting graves in Madinah:** Permissible for men to visit Al-Baqi' and the Prophet's ﷺ grave — for the purpose of making du'a to Allah and taking a lesson. It is NOT permissible to make du'a to or through the deceased — this constitutes shirk.

---

## TAHARAH (Ritual Purity)

### Categories of Najasah (Ritual Impurity)

- **Mughallazah (Heavy):** From dogs and pigs — 7 washes with one using soil (agreed between Shafi'i and Hanbali; based on sahih hadith)
- **Mutawassitah (Medium):** Blood, urine, feces, etc. — wash until completely removed
- **Mukhaffafah (Light):** Urine of a male infant before solid food — sprinkling water sufficient (Shafi'i and Salafi both agree)

### Actions That Nullify Wudhu — Shafi'i First

1. Anything exiting from the front or back private parts
2. Deep sleep (loss of discrimination)
3. Loss of consciousness
4. **Touching the private parts directly:**
   - **Shafi'i (mu'tamad):** Nullifies wudhu — whether with or without desire. (*Minhaj Al-Talibin*)
   - **Alternative (Hanbali/some Salafi):** Only nullifies with desire; without desire = no nullification
5. **Touching non-mahram skin:**
   - **Shafi'i (mu'tamad):** Nullifies wudhu (as noted in Salah section above)
   - **Stronger daleel:** Does not nullify without desire (Al-Albani position)
6. **Eating camel meat:**
   - **Shafi'i:** Does NOT nullify wudhu
   - **Salafi/Hanbali (stronger daleel):** DOES nullify wudhu — based on the explicit command of the Prophet ﷺ: *"Perform wudhu after eating camel meat."* (HR. Muslim no. 360 — sahih)
   - *Note: This is a case where the Salafi/Hanbali position has stronger hadith support. Those following Salafi methodology should renew wudhu after camel meat.*

**Reference:** *Minhaj Al-Talibin* (Imam Nawawi); *Fath Al-Mu'in*; islamqa.info; Rumaysho.com

# Muamalah Reference — Islamic Transactions (Manhaj Salaf)

Specialist scholar: **Ustaz Erwandi Tarmizi** | Primary reference: PengusahaMuslim.com, islamqa.info

---

## RIBA (Interest/Usury) — Strictly Prohibited

### Types of Riba

1. **Riba Al-Fadl** — An unequal exchange of ribawi commodities of the same type (e.g., gold for gold of unequal weight, wheat for wheat in unequal quantity)
2. **Riba An-Nasi'ah** — An addition charged in exchange for a delay in debt repayment; this is the basis of conventional bank interest

### Modern Applications

- Conventional bank savings accounts and loans bearing interest = **haram**
- Credit card interest charges = **haram**
- Installment sale with a price increase: **permissible** if the final price is fixed and agreed upon at the time of the contract (some ikhtilaf exists; this is the majority Salafi position)

**Key fatwa:** islamqa.info fatwa no. 13973

---

## GHARAR (Excessive Uncertainty) — Prohibited in Contracts

- Commercial insurance (Ta'min Tijari) = **haram** due to gharar (uncertainty) and maysir (gambling-like elements)
- Exception: Takaful (Islamic cooperative insurance) — permissible if structured correctly according to Shari'ah principles
- Futures trading involving commodities that cannot be delivered at the time of contract = generally **haram**

---

## AKAD (Contracts) — Conditions for Validity

An Islamic contract requires:

1. A clear offer (ijab) and acceptance (qabul)
2. Legally competent parties
3. A known and defined subject matter — no excessive uncertainty (gharar)
4. A permissible purpose and object

---

## COMMON MODERN FINANCIAL QUESTIONS

### Multi-Level Marketing (MLM)

- If income primarily derives from recruitment fees rather than actual product sales = **haram** (maysir and gharar)
- If structured around legitimate products with commissions tied to real sales = may be permissible; requires analysis of the specific scheme
- **Reference:** Ustaz Erwandi Tarmizi (PengusahaMuslim.com)

### Cryptocurrency

- Scholarly ikhtilaf exists; most Salafi scholars advise caution or prohibition due to:
  - Highly speculative nature (resembles maysir)
  - No intrinsic commodity backing
  - Extreme gharar (price volatility and uncertainty)
- **Reference:** islamqa.info fatwa no. 360668; positions vary among contemporary scholars <!-- TODO(maintainer): verify 360668 and 504160; confirm gharar/maysir wording -->

### Dropshipping / E-Commerce

- Selling goods one does not yet own before taking possession: scholarly ikhtilaf
- Salam contract (advance payment for specifically described future goods): **permissible** with defined conditions
- **Reference:** KonsultasiSyariah.com; islamqa.info

### Stock Market Investing

- Purchasing shares in companies whose primary business is halal = **permissible**
- Purchasing shares in companies primarily engaged in haram activities (conventional banking, alcohol, tobacco, etc.) = **haram**
- Short-selling = **haram** (selling what one does not own)

---

## ZAKAT ON BUSINESS ASSETS

- **Zakat Al-Tijarah (Trade Zakat):** 2.5% on net zakatable trade assets held for a full lunar year at or above the nisab threshold
- Nisab reference: equivalent to 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver — use the silver standard as it is more conservative and protective for zakat recipients

# references/tazkiyah.md

# Tazkiyatun Nafs Reference — Spiritual Purification (Manhaj Salaf)

Purification of the soul according to the Quran, Sunnah, and the understanding of the Salaf — free from Sufi innovations and unauthorized practices.

---

## FOUNDATION: Tazkiyah Is Achieved Through the Shari'ah

In the Salafi methodology, purification of the soul is not a secret path or mystical discipline — it is accomplished through:

1. Correct aqeedah (sound belief in Allah)
2. Performing obligatory and supererogatory acts of worship
3. Adhering to the Sunnah in all affairs — inwardly and outwardly
4. Avoiding sin and making sincere tawbah (repentance)
5. Keeping the company of righteous and knowledgeable people

**Not through:** Sufi dhikr circles, tariqa initiation or bay'ah to a spiritual guide, veneration of saints, or dhikr formulas invented outside of authenticated adhkar.

---

## KEY DISEASES OF THE HEART

### Riya' (Showing Off in Worship)

- Classified as minor shirk when present in acts of worship
- **Cure:** Constantly renew one's intention (niyyah) for the sake of Allah alone; remember that Allah alone truly witnesses every action
- **Reference:** islamqa.info; Ustaz Muhammad Nuzul Dzikri

### Kibr (Arrogance)

- The Prophet ﷺ defined it as: *"Rejecting the truth and belittling people."* (HR. Muslim no. 91)
- **Daleel:** *"He who has in his heart the weight of a mustard seed of arrogance shall not enter Paradise."* (HR. Muslim no. 91)
- **Cure:** Reflect deeply on one's origin (created from clay), one's sins before Allah, and the certainty of standing before Him on the Day of Judgment

### Hasad (Envy)

- **Haram form:** Wishing that a blessing Allah has given to another person be removed from them
- **Permissible form:** Ghibtah — wishing to have the same blessing for oneself, without wishing its removal from another
- **Cure:** Make du'a for the person you feel envious of; remind yourself that Allah distributes rizq (provision) according to His infinite wisdom

### Hubbud Dunya (Excessive Love of This World)

- **Clarification on zuhd (asceticism):** The Sunnah-based definition is not about making halal things haram or abandoning wealth — it means placing greater trust and confidence in what Allah has than in what one possesses
- *"Zuhd is not to make haram what Allah made halal, nor to waste wealth; rather zuhd is to have more confidence in what Allah has than in what you have."* (Reported by Ibn Majah — chain discussed by scholars)

## AUTHENTICATED ADHKAR (Daily Remembrance)

### Morning (After Fajr) and Evening (After Asr) Adhkar

Key authenticated supplications to recite:

- **Ayat Al-Kursi:** 1 time after each obligatory prayer
- **Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas:** 3 times each in the morning and evening
- **Sayyidul Istighfar:** 1 time in the morning and 1 time in the evening
- Morning opening supplication: *"Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna..."*

**Key reference book:** *Hisn Al-Muslim* (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa'id bin Ali Al-Qahtani — a verified compilation of authenticated adhkar from the Quran and Sunnah

### Adhkar After Obligatory Prayer

- SubhanAllah × 33, Alhamdulillah × 33, Allahu Akbar × 33
- Followed by: *"La ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul mulku wa lahul hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir"* × 1
- **Daleel:** HR. Muslim no. 597

## TAWBAH (Repentance)

### Four Conditions for a Valid Tawbah

1. **Ceasing the sin** immediately
2. **Remorse (nadam)** — genuine regret for having committed it
3. **Firm resolution** not to return to the sin
4. **If the sin involved the rights of others:** Return what was wrongly taken, or seek the person's pardon

**Daleel:** *"Indeed Allah accepts the repentance of the servant as long as the death rattle has not yet reached the throat."* (HR. At-Tirmidhi — graded hasan)

**Good news:** The door of tawbah is always open before one reaches the point of death.

## QIYAMUL LAYL (Night Prayer)

- One of the most highly praised voluntary acts of worship
- **Minimum:** 2 rak'at after Isha and before Fajr
- **Witr:** At minimum 1 rak'at; considered obligatory by some scholars, a strong sunnah by the majority — do not neglect it
- **Best time:** The last third of the night, when Allah descends (in a manner befitting His majesty) and calls out to His servants

**Daleel:** The Prophet ﷺ said: *"The best prayer after the obligatory ones is the night prayer (qiyamul layl)."* (HR. Muslim no. 1163)

## MAINTAINING SALAFI METHODOLOGY IN TAZKIYAH

Avoid the following as they have no basis in the Sunnah:

- Seeking guidance through dreams or visions
- Aspiring to "maqamat" (mystical spiritual stations) beyond what is recognized as taqwa and righteous deeds
- Exclusive dhikr circles with innovated formulas or counts
- Giving bay'ah (pledge of allegiance) to a spiritual guide outside of legitimate scholarly teaching relationships

**The correct path:** Seek beneficial Islamic knowledge, act upon it, worship Allah consistently, remain patient in difficulty, and do not despair of His mercy.
