Pass if the response has all of these:

1. A daleel section (a Quran verse or hadith, or a reference to one). Evidence quoted there may mention which side relies on it; that does not count as a position.
2. A positions section in which the Shafi'i position is the first madhab position stated. The order of madhabs inside that section is what matters; the daleel section is not part of the order.
3. A practical guidance section for the questioner. A short closing phrase such as "Wallahu a'lam" or a referral to a scholar after it is fine.

Fail if a non-Shafi'i madhab position is stated before the Shafi'i one in the positions section, if the Shafi'i position is missing, or if a required section is missing. Do not judge whether the positions are religiously correct.

Also pass only if this holds. When the positions differ, the position backed by stronger daleel (if the response presents one) comes after the Shafi'i position, each position is clearly labeled with its madhab or scholar, and the Shafi'i position is identified as the mu'tamad (relied-upon) position. If the response says the positions agree, this holds when the Shafi'i position is still labeled mu'tamad. Fail if the Shafi'i position is not identified as mu'tamad or relied-upon, or if positions are unlabeled.
